# Retail Sales — Findings
Dataset: 2,400 synthetic transactions covering 2024-01-01 to 2024-12-31.

- Total sales: **R12,459,956.61**
- Total recorded profit: **R2,263,074.48**
- Average transaction value: **R5,191.65**
- Highest-sales category: **Computers** (R5,209,233.97)
- Highest-sales region: **Gauteng** (R5,049,664.95)
- Peak month: **2024-04** (R1,182,905.98)

## Interpretation
Category and regional totals identify where revenue is concentrated. Monthly movement provides a baseline for seasonality and operational planning. These are descriptive findings from generated demonstration data—not evidence about a real retailer.

## Recommended next analyses
1. Compare profit margin by product and region, not sales alone.
2. Investigate discount levels against margin and order volume.
3. Add customer IDs and inventory data to examine repeat purchases and stock-outs.
