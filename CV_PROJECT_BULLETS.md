# CV-ready project descriptions (adapt after you understand and can explain every step)

- **Retail Sales Analysis | SQL, Python, Data Visualization:** Queried and analyzed 2,400 transaction records; built category, regional and monthly sales summaries; visualized revenue patterns and documented actionable business questions.
- **Customer Churn Prediction | Python, scikit-learn:** Built a reproducible Random Forest classification pipeline with categorical encoding, stratified train/test split and class weighting; assessed accuracy, precision, recall, F1 and ROC-AUC; documented model limitations and responsible use.
- **Energy Demand Analysis | Python, pandas, matplotlib:** Explored 8,760 hourly observations; charted monthly and hourly demand profiles and examined temperature-demand association; translated exploratory findings into capacity-planning considerations.
